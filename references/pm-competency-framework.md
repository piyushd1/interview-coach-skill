# PM & Business Operator Competency Framework

This framework defines the key competencies tested in Product Management, Business Operator (P&L leader), Strategy, and General Management interviews at tech companies and startups. Used by `stories find gaps`, `prep`, `decode`, and `progress` for competency-based analysis.

---

## How to Use

- **Gap Analysis**: Map storybank stories against these competencies. A story's Primary Skill maps to one competency; Secondary Skills may cover others. See `references/story-mapping-engine.md` for fit scoring.
- **Question Prediction**: When preparing for a company, predict which competencies will be tested based on role type, JD analysis, and company stage.
- **Story Building**: When building new stories, target uncovered competencies — especially those marked "Critical" for the candidate's target roles.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Product Sense & User Empathy

**What interviewers test**: Can you identify the right problem? Do you understand users deeply, or do you rely on data and assumptions?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Problem identification and framing
- User segmentation and persona thinking
- Balancing user needs vs. business goals
- Product intuition with evidence backing
- Saying "no" to features with conviction

**Question patterns**:
- "How would you improve [product]?"
- "Tell me about a time you identified a user need that wasn't obvious from the data."
- "How do you decide what NOT to build?"
- "Walk me through how you prioritize features."

**Priority by role**: PM ★★★ | Business Head ★★ | Strategy ★ | Operator ★★

---

### 2. Prioritization & Roadmap Strategy

**What interviewers test**: Can you choose what to build (and kill) with clear reasoning? Can you manage competing priorities from multiple stakeholders?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Framework usage (RICE, ICE, etc.) AND knowing when frameworks fail
- Stakeholder trade-off navigation
- Saying "no" to powerful stakeholders with data
- Roadmap communication and buy-in
- Balancing short-term wins vs. long-term bets

**Question patterns**:
- "Tell me about a difficult prioritization decision."
- "How did you handle conflicting priorities from engineering, sales, and leadership?"
- "Tell me about a time you killed a feature or project."
- "How do you balance tech debt against feature development?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★★ | Business Head ★★ | Strategy ★★ | Operator ★★

---

### 3. P&L Ownership & Financial Acumen

**What interviewers test**: Do you think like a business owner? Can you speak in revenue, margins, and unit economics — not just features and user metrics?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Revenue and cost structure understanding
- Unit economics design and optimization
- Pricing strategy and experimentation
- Financial modeling and forecasting
- Budget allocation and resource trade-offs
- ROI-driven investment decisions

**Question patterns**:
- "Walk me through the P&L for a business you owned."
- "How did you think about pricing for your product?"
- "Tell me about a time you improved unit economics."
- "How did you justify the investment in [project/team]?"
- "How did you balance growth vs. profitability?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★ | Business Head ★★★ | Strategy ★★★ | Operator ★★★

---

### 4. Strategic Thinking & Market Analysis

**What interviewers test**: Can you see the big picture? Can you identify market opportunities, competitive threats, and make smart bets?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Market sizing and opportunity assessment (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- Competitive analysis and positioning
- Portfolio strategy and resource allocation across bets
- Market entry and expansion strategy
- Pattern recognition across multiple contexts
- First-principles thinking vs. following playbooks

**Question patterns**:
- "How would you enter [market]?"
- "What do you think about [industry trend]?"
- "Tell me about a strategic bet you made. What was the thesis?"
- "How did you decide which markets/segments to focus on?"
- "What's your framework for evaluating opportunities?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★ | Business Head ★★★ | Strategy ★★★ | Operator ★★

---

### 5. Technical Fluency & System Design Communication

**What interviewers test**: Can you communicate with engineers as equals? Can you make architecture decisions as a PM? Do you understand the technical trade-offs of your product decisions?

**Sub-competencies**:
- System design communication (narrating trade-offs, not whiteboarding)
- Build vs. buy decision-making
- AI/ML product thinking (LLM deployment, model selection, evaluation)
- Data pipeline and analytics architecture understanding
- Technical debt assessment and prioritization
- Scoping and estimation collaboration with engineering

**Question patterns**:
- "Walk me through a technical architecture decision you made."
- "How did you decide to build vs. buy [component]?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to push back on engineering's approach."
- "How do you evaluate AI/ML solutions for product problems?"
- "Walk me through how you'd design [system] for [use case]."

**Priority by role**: PM ★★★ | Business Head ★ | Strategy ★ | Operator ★★

---

### 6. GTM Execution & Growth

**What interviewers test**: Can you launch products that find users/customers? Do you understand acquisition, activation, retention, monetization?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Launch planning and execution
- Growth loops and acquisition channel strategy
- Funnel optimization (acquisition → activation → retention → monetization)
- Partnership and distribution strategy
- A/B testing and experimentation rigor
- Scaling what works (and killing what doesn't)

**Question patterns**:
- "How did you launch [product]? What was the GTM strategy?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to find product-market fit."
- "How did you acquire your first 100/1000/10000 users?"
- "Walk me through a growth experiment that surprised you."

**Priority by role**: PM ★★ | Business Head ★★★ | Strategy ★★ | Operator ★★★

---

### 7. C-Suite Communication & Executive Influence

**What interviewers test**: Can you influence senior leadership? Can you translate product/technical strategy into business language? Can you handle pushback from powerful stakeholders?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Executive presentation skills (conclusion-first, data-backed)
- Managing up (keeping leadership informed without micromanagement)
- Board/leadership communication
- Influencing without authority at the executive level
- Handling executive pushback with composure and data
- Framing bad news constructively

**Question patterns**:
- "Tell me about a time you influenced a senior leader's decision."
- "How do you communicate product strategy to the board/C-suite?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news to leadership."
- "How did you get buy-in for an unpopular decision?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★ | Business Head ★★★ | Strategy ★★★ | Operator ★★

---

### 8. Cross-Functional Leadership & Influence Without Authority

**What interviewers test**: Can you get things done through others? Can you align engineering, design, sales, operations, and marketing without having direct authority?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Aligning multiple teams toward shared goals
- Navigating disagreements constructively
- Building trust across functions
- Running effective cross-functional processes (reviews, sprints, launches)
- Managing dependencies across teams

**Question patterns**:
- "Tell me about a time you had to influence a decision without direct authority."
- "How do you handle disagreements with engineering/design/sales?"
- "Tell me about a time a cross-functional project went off track. What did you do?"
- "How do you build trust with a new team?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★★ | Business Head ★★ | Strategy ★★ | Operator ★★★

---

### 9. Marketplace & Platform Dynamics

**What interviewers test**: Do you understand two-sided marketplace mechanics? Can you balance supply and demand? Do you understand network effects and liquidity?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Supply-demand balancing and marketplace liquidity
- Chicken-and-egg problem solving
- Network effects and flywheel design
- Supply quality and curation
- Marketplace pricing and take rate strategy
- Disintermediation prevention

**Question patterns**:
- "How would you bootstrap a marketplace in a new city?"
- "How do you balance supply quality vs. supply quantity?"
- "Tell me about a marketplace dynamic that surprised you."
- "How do you handle the chicken-and-egg problem?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★ (marketplace roles ★★★) | Business Head ★★ | Strategy ★★ | Operator ★★★ (marketplace operators)

---

### 10. Team Building & People Leadership

**What interviewers test**: Can you build, grow, and manage teams? Can you hire well, fire when needed, and develop people?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Hiring philosophy and execution
- Team structure and org design
- Performance management and difficult conversations
- Developing and mentoring PMs
- Culture building
- Delegation and empowerment

**Question patterns**:
- "How do you build a PM team?"
- "Tell me about someone you hired who didn't work out."
- "How do you handle underperformance?"
- "What's your management philosophy?"
- "How do you set OKRs/goals for your team?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★ (GPM/Director ★★★) | Business Head ★★★ | Strategy ★ | Operator ★★★

---

### 11. 0-to-1 Building & Entrepreneurship

**What interviewers test**: Can you build from nothing? Can you handle extreme ambiguity and missing infrastructure?

**Sub-competencies**:
- Idea validation and lean experimentation
- MVP scoping and launch
- Resourcefulness and frugal innovation
- Pivoting based on market feedback
- Building without playbooks or existing processes

**Question patterns**:
- "Tell me about a time you built something from scratch."
- "How do you validate an idea before investing engineering resources?"
- "Tell me about a project where you had no playbook to follow."
- "How did you handle the ambiguity of building something new?"

**Priority by role**: PM ★★ | Business Head ★★★ | Strategy ★★ | Operator ★★★

---

### 12. Failure, Learning & Self-Awareness

**What interviewers test**: Can you own mistakes? Do you have the self-awareness to recognize what went wrong and what you'd do differently? This is a universal competency — every role tests it.

**Sub-competencies**:
- Genuine failure ownership (not "challenge I overcame")
- Root cause analysis of personal mistakes
- Behavioral change from lessons learned
- Intellectual honesty about limitations
- Growth trajectory narration

**Question patterns**:
- "Tell me about your biggest failure."
- "Tell me about a time you were wrong."
- "What would you do differently if you could redo [project]?"
- "What's the hardest feedback you've received? What did you do with it?"

**Priority by role**: ALL ROLES ★★★

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is the most commonly under-prepared competency. Candidates default to "challenge I overcame" stories, which interviewers see through immediately. Must have at least one genuine failure story with real consequences and real learning. See `references/story-seeds.md` — Critical Gaps section.
